# Temple facts — verified research

Researched 2026-08-16. The point of this file is to separate **what is actually attested**
(inscriptions, dated construction, documented events, GI registrations) from **what is
tradition** (puranic legend, devotional claim, "dates back 3,000 years"). Both belong in a
piece like this — but they should not be dressed the same way.

Marked throughout:

- **Attested** — inscriptions, dated records, documented events, official registrations.
- **Traditional** — devotional or puranic claim. True as tradition, not as history.
- **Disputed** — sources genuinely conflict; stated as such rather than resolved.

A caution learned doing this: temple history online is heavily copy-pasted and often wrong.
One widely-repeated page claims Varadaraja Perumal was "built by the Vijayanagara dynasty in
the 12th century" — impossible, as Vijayanagara was founded in 1336. Anything below without a
marker is something I could not corroborate and you should treat as unverified.

## Kanchipuram

### KAN · Ekambareswarar
- **Built** *(attested)* — Pallava origin under Mahendravarman I, 7th c., in brick and stone.
  Rebuilt in granite by the Cholas, 9th c., with a hundred-pillared hall.
- **Attested** — the nine-storey south gopuram, ~59 m (192 ft), was raised by **Krishnadevaraya
  of Vijayanagara in 1509**; he and his queen are carved on it. Thousand-pillar hall also
  Vijayanagara. Complex covers ~23 acres.
- **Famous for** — the Prithvi (earth) lingam of the Pancha Bhoota Sthalams; one of the tallest
  gopurams in South India.
- **Traditional** — the mango tree said to be 3,000+ years old bearing four different fruits.
  This is the temple's signature story and worth keeping, but as legend.

### VAR · Varadaraja Perumal
- **Built** — Pallava origins, Chola rebuilding, major expansion under Vijayanagara (the
  hundred-pillared hall with its chain-link carving). **Not** a 12th-century Vijayanagara
  foundation, despite many sources saying so.
- **Attested** — **Athi Varadar**, the fig-wood icon kept submerged in the temple tank, is
  raised for worship once every 40 years for 48 days. Rediscovered in **1709** when the tank
  was emptied. Raised 1939, 1979, and 1 July–17 Aug **2019**. Next: **2059**.
- **Famous for** — a Divya Desam; the Athi Varadar darshan draws millions.

### KAM · Kamakshi Amman
- **Built** — Pallava-era foundation (commonly given as 6th–8th c.), later rebuilding.
- **Traditional** — Adi Shankara installing the Sri Chakra and calming the goddess's fierce
  form; the seat of the Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham.
- **Famous for** — the Shiva temples of Kanchi keep no separate goddess shrine, so all worship
  of the goddess flows here. **Note:** two different triads circulate — Kamakshi with Madurai
  and Varanasi (pan-Indian), and Kamakshi with Meenakshi (Madurai) and Akilandeswari
  (Thiruvanaikaval) as the three great goddess centres *of Tamil Nadu*. The current app text
  uses the first; the second is the more commonly cited one in Tamil sources.

**Kanchipuram, to eat and do** — Kanchipuram silk sarees (GI-registered) are the town's other
pilgrimage. **Kanchipuram idli**, mildly spiced and cylindrical, is the prasadam at Varadaraja
Perumal.

## The northern shrines

### THI · Thiruttani Murugan
- One of the six **Arupadai Veedu**, named in Sangam-era literature via Nakkeerar's
  *Thirumurugatrupadai* and later Arunagirinathar's *Thiruppugazh*.
- **Famous for** — 365 steps to the shrine, one per day of the year; Aadi Krittikai.
- *Dating is thin in accessible sources — flagged as a gap below.*

### TVM · Arunachaleswarar, Thiruvannamalai
- **Built** *(attested)* — present structure dates from the **9th c. per inscriptions**, built
  under the Cholas, who patronised it for four centuries; Pallava presence before that.
  Expanded under Vijayanagara (including Krishnadevaraya), then the Saluva and Tuluva dynasties.
- **Attested** — ~25 acres, nine gopurams, thousand-pillared hall.
- **Famous for** — the Agni (fire) lingam; **Karthigai Deepam**, when a beacon is lit on the
  summit; the 14 km barefoot **girivalam** around the hill on full-moon nights. Ramana Maharshi
  lived at the mountain.

## The Chola country

### CHI · Nataraja Temple, Chidambaram
- **Built** *(attested)* — present structure mainly **10th–13th c.** The Cholas held Nataraja as
  their family deity; **Parantaka I** gilded the sanctum roof in gold; Rajaraja I and Kulottunga I
  made major endowments. ~40 acres.
- **Attested** — the **108 karanas** of Bharatanatyam carved on the east gopuram; the hereditary
  **Dikshitar** priests still serve the shrine.
- **Traditional** — origins "in the 2nd century BC" / Sangam period. Devotional dating, not
  archaeological.
- **Famous for** — the *Chidambara Rahasyam*, the empty space behind the curtain: the god as
  formless ether, the space element of the Pancha Bhoota.

### GCP · Gangaikonda Cholapuram
- **Built** *(attested)* — completed **c. 1035 CE by Rajendra Chola I** as the shrine of his new
  capital, which served as the Chola capital from **1025 to 1279**.
- **Attested** — built to mark his campaign to the Ganges; he took the title *Gangaikonda Cholan*
  and filled the **Chola Gangam** tank with Ganges water. **UNESCO** Great Living Chola Temples.
- **Famous for** — the vimana curves gently inward where Thanjavur's rises straight; deliberately
  conceived as companion and rival to his father's temple 70 km away.

### THA · Brihadeeswarar, Thanjavur
- **Built** *(attested)* — by **Rajaraja Chola I, 1003–1010 CE**. Begun in his 19th regnal year,
  finished on the 275th day of his 25th.
- **Attested** — vimana over **60 m (200 ft)**, representing Mount Meru; built of granite quarried
  ~60 km away near Tiruchirappalli; extensive **Tamil inscriptions** and frescoes.
  **UNESCO World Heritage, inscribed 1987.**
- **To do nearby** — Thanjavur is known for its **art plates** and Thanjavur painting; the sweet
  **chandrakala**.

### DAR · Airavatesvara, Darasuram
- **Built** *(attested)* — by **Rajaraja Chola II** (reigned **1143–1173**), 12th c.
- **Attested** — 80-ft vimana built as a **chariot drawn by horses**; famous micro-carvings, some
  under an inch high; the "musical steps." Under **ASI** care since **1954**. **UNESCO.**
- Smaller than Thanjavur and Gangaikondacholapuram, and finer.

### KUM · Adi Kumbeswarar, Kumbakonam
- **Attested** — centre of **Mahamaham**, held **every 12 years** when Jupiter in Leo coincides
  with the full moon in Leo, in the Tamil month of Masi (Feb–Mar). Draws roughly **2 million**
  pilgrims. The rite runs: dips in the 20 wells, the Kumbeswarar shrine, the Mahamaham tank,
  then the Cauvery.
- **To eat** — **Kumbakonam degree coffee**, the real thing rather than the roadside brand; and
  **idli with kadappa**, the town's own coconut-and-dal gravy.

### SWA · Swaminathaswamy, Swamimalai
- Fourth of the **Arupadai Veedu**.
- **Traditional** — Murugan expounding the meaning of *Om* to Shiva, and so taking the name
  Swaminatha, teacher of his own father.
- **To do** — Swamimalai is the centre of **traditional lost-wax bronze icon casting**, a craft
  still practised by resident sthapathi families and GI-recognised.

### TVK · Jambukeswarar, Thiruvanaikaval
- **Attested** — the **Appu (water) lingam** of the Pancha Bhoota, with a spring in the sanctum
  that never dries. Five enclosures; the outer **Vibhuti Prakara** wall runs over a mile.
- **Traditional** — built by Kochengat Chola; the "2nd century AD" dating is legendary. Later
  Pandya, Vijayanagara and Nayaka additions are real.

### SRI · Ranganathaswamy, Srirangam
- **Attested** — **156 acres**, the **largest functioning Hindu temple complex in the world**;
  foremost of the **108 Divya Desams**. On the **UNESCO tentative list**.
- **Attested** — sacked by the Delhi Sultanate, with Arabic sources recording invasions in
  **1311 and 1323**; restored under **Vijayanagara**. The fabric carries Chola, Pandya, Hoysala,
  Kongu and Vijayanagara work.

### SAM · Samayapuram Mariamman
- **Disputed / thin** — early history unclear. The present temple was built in the **early 18th c.
  by Vijayaraya Chakravarti II**; the area had been a Vijayanagara army base in the 17th c.
  Local worship is believed to long predate the building.
- **Famous for** — one of the wealthiest temples in Tamil Nadu; the Poochoriyal festival.

## The Navagraha nine

VAI · SUR · TVE · KEE · THL · KAJ · TNG · ALA · TIN

- **Attested as a group** — a cluster of Chola-era shrines, **9th–12th c.**, spread within roughly
  **60–80 km** around Kumbakonam and Mayiladuthurai, each assigned to one of the nine grahas.
- **SUR · Suryanar Kovil** — the rare one where **all nine grahas have their own shrines**.
  Commonly dated to **Kulottunga Chola I, c. 1100**, on a foundation inscription.
- **THL · Thirunallar** (Shani) — passed with Karaikal into **French India**, and is today in
  **Puducherry**, not Tamil Nadu. The Nala theertham bath before darshan. *Sani peyarchi*, when
  Saturn changes house, draws enormous crowds.
- **Individual dating for the remaining seven is genuinely thin** in accessible sources — see gaps.

## Madurai and the south

### MAD · Meenakshi Amman, Madurai
- **Attested** — Pandya-era origins; **sacked by Malik Kafur in 1310**, destroying the older
  fabric. Rebuilding was begun by **Viswanatha Nayak** (mid-16th c.) and the temple was greatly
  enlarged by **Thirumalai Nayak, who ruled 1623–1655**.
- **Attested** — **14 gopurams**, essentially all **16th–17th c.**; complex covers ~6 hectares.
- **Traditional** — the "2,500 years old" claim. The site is ancient; the building is Nayak.
- **To eat** — **jigarthanda** ("cool heart"), of milk, almond gum, nannari syrup and ice cream.
  Madurai **malli** (jasmine) is GI-registered.

### TPK · Thiruparankundram
- **Built** *(attested)* — an **8th-c. early Pandya rock-cut cave temple**, the sanctum carved
  from living rock, with later work continuing to the 13th c. and Nayak additions.
- **Attested** — Shiva and Vishnu shrines **face each other**, which is unusual to the point of
  near-unique.
- **Traditional** — the "2nd century BC" dating that circulates.

### AZK · Kallazhagar (Azhagar Kovil)
- **Attested** — a Divya Desam praised in the *Silappadikaram* and the *Divya Prabandham*, so
  known by the Sangam era. Under the Pandyas c. **1251–1563**; the Kalyana Mandapam with its yali
  pillars is **Vijayanagara–Nayak** work.
- **Famous for** — the Chithirai festival, when Kallazhagar rides down to enter the river Vaigai.

### PAZ · Pazhamudircholai
- Sixth of the **Arupadai Veedu**, in forest above Madurai.
- **Traditional** — Murugan testing the poet Avvaiyar with the riddle of the ripe fruit.

### PAL · Palani Dhandayuthapani
- **Traditional** — foundation in the 9th c. under the Chera king Cheraman Perumal; the idol
  fashioned of *navapashanam* by the siddhar Bogar. Both are devotional tradition.
- **Attested** — **Palani panchamirtham was the first temple prasadam in India to receive a
  Geographical Indication tag** (2019). Banana, jaggery, cow ghee, honey and cardamom, with dates
  and sugar candy. Anointed on the idol first, then mixed back and given out.

### SVP · Andal Temple, Srivilliputhur
- **Attested** — a Divya Desam; home of **Andal**, the only woman among the twelve Alvars, author
  of the *Thiruppavai* and *Nachiyar Thirumozhi*, placed in the 7th–8th c. Rajagopuram of about
  **192 ft in 11 tiers**, among the tallest in India.
- **Disputed — and a good story** — the gopuram is very widely said to be the model for the
  **Tamil Nadu state emblem**. The emblem's designer, Krishna Rao, **denied this and said it was
  the west gopuram of the Meenakshi temple**. Worth telling *as* the dispute.
- **To eat** — **Srivilliputhur palkova** received its **GI tag on 10 September 2019**; made by a
  cooperative operating since 1945, still cooked manually in iron vessels over firewood.

### RAM · Ramanathaswamy, Rameswaram
- **Attested** — one of the **twelve Jyotirlingas**. 12th-c. sanctum work associated with
  **Parakramabahu I of Polonnaruwa** (Sri Lanka). The **third corridor was built under
  Muthuramalinga Sethupathi in 1769**, with **1212 pillars**.
- **Correction needed** — the third corridor measures about **197 m east–west by 133 m
  north–south** and is usually called **Asia's** longest temple corridor. The "1.2 km" figure in
  the current app text is the *combined* length of the corridors, not a single one, and "longest
  in the world" overstates it.
- **Famous for** — the 22 theerthams (wells) within, each said to taste different; sea-bathing at
  Agni theertham.

### TCH · Thiruchendur
- **Attested** — the only one of the six abodes on the seashore; the gopuram unusually rises to
  the **west**.
- **Attested** — occupied by the **Dutch East India Company, 1646–1648**, during their war with
  Portugal. On withdrawing they removed the principal images to **Galle** in Dutch Ceylon and
  **held them for ransom**. They were **returned and reinstalled in January 1651**; a panchaloka
  replacement had been made in the interim.
- **Correction needed** — the current app text says the bronzes were "recovered from the sea."
  That is the devotional version. The documented account is ransom and negotiated return.

### KKM · Bhagavathi Amman, Kanyakumari
- **Attested** — a **Shakti Peetha**; long part of the **Travancore** kingdom; stands at the
  confluence of the Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea and Indian Ocean.
- **Attested** — **Vivekananda meditated on the offshore rock in 1892**; the **Vivekananda Rock
  Memorial was built in 1970**.
- **Traditional** — the "3,000 years old" dating, and the diamond nose-ring so bright it misled
  ships.

## Corrections to make in the current app data

1. **TCH Thiruchendur** — "recovered from the sea" is legend. Documented: held for ransom in
   Galle, returned January 1651.
2. **RAM Rameswaram** — "the longest temple corridor in the world, near 1.2 kilometres" conflates
   the combined corridors with the third corridor, and overstates the rank. Use: Asia's longest,
   ~197 × 133 m, 1212 pillars, built 1769.
3. **MAD Madurai** — the temple is presented as ancient; the *building* is Nayak, 16th–17th c.,
   after Malik Kafur's sack in 1310. Worth saying, it is a better story than "very old."
4. **CHI Chidambaram / TVK Thiruvanaikaval / KKM Kanyakumari** — the very early datings in
   circulation are devotional. Present as tradition.
5. **KAN Ekambareswarar** — the 1509 Krishnadevaraya gopuram is solid and already correct; the
   3,000-year mango tree should read as legend.
6. **KAM Kamakshi** — check which triad you mean; the Tamil Nadu triad (with Meenakshi and
   Akilandeswari) is the more usual one in Tamil sources.
7. **SVP Srivilliputhur** — the state-emblem connection is worth adding *and* worth telling as
   disputed, since the designer denied it.

## Gaps still to research

Individual dating and attribution for **THI Thiruttani**, **PAZ Pazhamudircholai**, **SWA
Swamimalai**, and **seven of the nine Navagraha shrines** (VAI, TVE, KEE, KAJ, TNG, ALA, TIN).
These have thin scholarly records online; doing them properly means epigraphic sources
(ASI inscription volumes, the *South Indian Inscriptions* series) rather than travel sites.
